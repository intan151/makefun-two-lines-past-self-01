Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Two Lines to Your Past Self"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca).

`main.py` is deterministic — no network, no randomness — and writes a single
two-line entry to `/output/entry.txt`: a warning to the writer's past self
about one specific moment (co-signing a cousin's motorbike loan outside the
bank on 12 June), and one sentence on why it mattered (the missed payments
became the writer's debt, paid off over two years for a bike never ridden).

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
